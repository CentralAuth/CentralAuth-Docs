# Get the CentralAuth app

The CentralAuth app is currently only available for download on Android devices. Head over to the Google Play Store to download and install the app on your device:

[<img src="/img/google_play.svg" alt="Get it on Google Play" width="200" />](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.centralauth.app)
